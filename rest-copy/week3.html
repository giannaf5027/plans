WEEK 03

Learning
to Listen

Hearing God's Voice in the Quiet

A quiet evening designed to help us practice listening, patience, and discernment.

KEY SCRIPTURES

John 10:27 & 1 Kings 19:11–13

DISCUSSION

Hearing God

  1. When you think about hearing God's voice, what comes to mind—clarity, confusion, fear, or uncertainty?
  2. Why do you think God often speaks in whispers rather than in dramatic ways?
  3. What makes it difficult to trust that what we're hearing is truly from God?
  4. How do emotions, expectations, or anxiety affect our ability to listen?
  5. In what ways can silence feel uncomfortable or vulnerable?
  6. How can Scripture help us discern God's voice from our own thoughts?
  7. What role does patience play in learning to hear God more clearly?
  8. How would your life look different if you truly believed God wanted to speak to you?

OUTDOOR SESSION

Depot Park / Lake Alice

This week works especially well outdoors. Find a quiet area near the water and allow the environment itself to create space for stillness.

ACTIVITY

Silent Prayer

  1. Find a comfortable place to sit.
  2. Begin by reading John 10:27 slowly.
  3. Spend 10–15 minutes in silence.
  4. Do not force yourself to receive a specific answer.
  5. If thoughts arise, acknowledge them and return your attention to God.
  6. End by writing down what you noticed.

Discernment Reminder

Not every thought, feeling, or impression we experience is automatically God's voice.

Scripture remains our primary standard for discerning what is true.
