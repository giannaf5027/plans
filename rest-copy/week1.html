WEEK 01

The Invitation
to Be Still

Starting with Intimacy

Beginning our journey by learning to slow down and simply be present with God.

KEY SCRIPTURES

Psalm 46 & Matthew 11:28–30

"Be still, and know that I am God."

Psalm 46:10

DISCUSSION

Questions

  1. When you hear the phrase "be still," what emotions or reactions come up for you? Peace, discomfort, resistance, curiosity?
  2. Why do you think God invites us to stillness before He invites us to action?
  3. What do you think intimacy with God actually looks like in real life—not in an ideal version, but in your everyday rhythm?
  4. How does busyness affect your relationship with God emotionally and spiritually?
  5. What are the biggest internal distractions that keep you from being fully present with God?
  6. Jesus says His yoke is easy and His burden is light. Why do you think following Him can still feel heavy at times?
  7. What fears or lies might surface when life slows down?
  8. If stillness is an invitation, not simply a command, what might God be inviting you into right now?

ACTIVITY

Silent Meditation & Reflection

10 Minutes of Stillness

  1. Find a comfortable position.
  2. Put away phones and distractions.
  3. Begin with a short prayer asking God to help you become present with Him.
  4. Sit quietly for approximately 10 minutes.
  5. Do not worry about producing a specific spiritual experience.
  6. Simply be present with God.

Afterward, journal any thoughts, feelings, prayers, distractions, or realizations that came up during the silence.

Personal Reflection

How often do I actually make space to simply be with God?

What makes stillness difficult for me?

What is one small way I can create space for God this week?
