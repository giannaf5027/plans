WEEK 04

Scripture

Letting God's Word Shape Us

Moving from simply reading Scripture to allowing God's Word to transform the way we think and live.

KEY SCRIPTURES

Psalm 1:1–3 & Joshua 1:8

DISCUSSION

Scripture & Transformation

  1. How has your relationship with Scripture changed over time?
  2. Why do we often approach the Bible looking primarily for answers?
  3. What does it mean to let God's Word shape us instead of simply inform us?
  4. How does Scripture slow us down in a culture that values speed and efficiency?
  5. What makes it difficult to stay engaged with Scripture consistently?
  6. How can Scripture become a source of comfort rather than pressure?
  7. What role do repetition and reflection play in spiritual growth?
  8. How might your thoughts, decisions, or reactions change if Scripture truly filled your heart?

ACTIVITY

Scripture Meditation & Reflection

Choose a Short Passage

Consider Psalm 23, a parable of Jesus, or another short passage of Scripture.

  1. Read the passage aloud slowly.
  2. Read it a second time.
  3. Notice any words or phrases that stand out.
  4. Sit silently for several minutes.
  5. Ask God what He may be showing you.
  6. Journal your observations and prayers.

Discussion After Meditation

How does meditating on God's Word slow us down and change the way we think?

What are some practical ways to keep Scripture in our hearts throughout the week?
