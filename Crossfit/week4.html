WEEK 04

Keep Your Eyes on Jesus

Focus Determines Direction

Like Peter walking on water, our circumstances may not immediately change. But where we place our focus changes how we respond.

Scripture

  • Hebrews 12:2
  • Matthew 14:22–33
  • Colossians 3:1–4
  • Philippians 4:8
  • Psalm 121:1–2
  • John 15:4–5

Discussion Questions

  1. What happens to Peter when he first gets out of the boat?
  2. What changes when Peter notices the wind and waves?
  3. Did Jesus move, or did Peter's focus change?
  4. What does it practically mean to “keep your eyes on Jesus”?
  5. What most easily distracts you from God?
  6. How can comparison affect your spiritual focus?
  7. How can social media affect what occupies your mind?
  8. What do you tend to focus on when you're stressed?
  9. What habits help you redirect your attention toward Christ?
  10. What distraction do you need to intentionally turn away from this week?

FIT — FOCUS

⚖️ Balance & Focus

Round 1

Stand on one leg while focusing on a fixed point.

Round 2

Look around while attempting to maintain balance.

Round 3

Close your eyes and attempt to maintain balance.

Reflection

When our focus changes, our stability changes.

Connect this to Peter: the storm didn't disappear. Peter's attention shifted.
