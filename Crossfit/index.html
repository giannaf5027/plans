CROSS + FIT

Faith in Motion

A Christ-centered Bible study exploring what it means to follow Jesus faithfully, run the race with endurance, and prepare ourselves for a life of discipleship.

“Let us run with endurance the race that lies before us, keeping our eyes on Jesus.” — Hebrews 12:1–2

The Idea

CROSS + FIT is built around one central idea: the Christian life is not a sprint to heaven. It is a lifelong marathon of faithfully following Jesus.

The Cross comes first. We do not train to earn God's love or salvation. We train because of what Christ has already done for us.

Our Core Message

We don't train to earn the Cross. We train because of the Cross.

THE CROSS + FIT FRAMEWORK

Five Ways We Follow the Cross

CROSS + FIT is more than a workout with a Bible study attached. Each session asks us to take something we learn about Christ and put it into motion.

C

Christ

Everything begins with Jesus and what He has accomplished for us through the Cross.

Ask:

What does this teach me about Christ?

R

Release

We surrender the weights, fears, distractions, and desires that keep us from faithfully following Jesus.

Ask:

What do I need to surrender to God?

O

Obedience

Faith is not simply knowing what God says. We respond by putting His Word into practice.

Ask:

What is Jesus asking me to do?

S

Strength

We recognize that our strength ultimately comes from God rather than from ourselves.

Ask:

Where do I need God's strength?

S

Solidarity

We do not run the Christian race alone. We encourage, carry, challenge, and strengthen one another in Christ.

Ask:

Who am I running alongside?

+

FIT

We put biblical truth into motion through physical activity, reflection, and community.

Ask:

How can I put this truth into motion?

WHAT IS CROSS + FIT?

Faith in Motion

CROSS + FIT is a Christ-centered Bible study designed around the idea that following Jesus is a lifelong race.

We aren't sprinting toward heaven trying to earn God's approval. We are running a marathon of faithful discipleship because Christ has already given Himself for us.

We Don't Train to Earn the Cross.

We train because of the Cross.

Through Scripture, discussion, prayer, and physical activity, we learn to keep our eyes on Christ, release what weighs us down, obey His calling, depend on His strength, and run alongside our sisters in Christ.
