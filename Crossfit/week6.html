WEEK 06

Train for Godliness

Discipline Over Motivation

Scripture

  • 1 Timothy 4:7–8
  • 1 Corinthians 9:24–27
  • Romans 12:1–2
  • John 15:4–5
  • 2 Peter 1:5–8
  • Psalm 1:1–3

Discussion Questions

  1. What does Paul mean by “train yourself in godliness”?
  2. Why does Paul compare physical training with spiritual training?
  3. What's the difference between motivation and discipline?
  4. What happens if we only pray or read Scripture when we feel like it?
  5. What spiritual disciplines help you remain connected to Christ?
  6. How can spiritual disciplines become legalistic?
  7. What's the difference between discipline to earn God's love and discipline because you already have God's love?
  8. What does consistency look like in your relationship with God?
  9. What spiritual habit do you know would benefit your relationship with Christ?
  10. What is one small spiritual habit you could faithfully practice for the next 30 days?

FIT — WORKOUT

🏋️ Rep by Rep

Complete as many quality rounds as possible in five minutes, working at your own pace.

  • 10 squats
  • 10 alternating lunges
  • 5–10 push-ups
  • 10 crunches
  • 20 jumping jacks

Reflection

One workout doesn't make you fit. One prayer doesn't make you spiritually mature. Growth comes through faithful repetition.

The goal is not speed or competition. The goal is consistency.
