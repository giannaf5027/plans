WEEK 05

Strength in Weakness

You Don't Have to Be Strong Enough

Scripture

  • 2 Corinthians 12:7–10
  • Isaiah 40:29–31
  • Psalm 46:1
  • Psalm 73:26
  • 2 Corinthians 4:7–9
  • Philippians 4:13

Discussion Questions

  1. Why do we often feel like we have to appear strong?
  2. What kinds of weakness do college students tend to hide?
  3. What does Paul mean when he says God's grace is sufficient?
  4. Why doesn't God simply remove Paul's weakness?
  5. How can weakness actually draw us closer to God?
  6. What's the difference between depending on God and refusing to take responsibility?
  7. Why can saying “I need help” be an act of faith?
  8. Where do you currently feel inadequate?
  9. Who can you be honest with about your struggles?
  10. What would change if you stopped viewing weakness as something you had to hide from God?

FIT — STRENGTH

💪 Plank to Your Limit

Everyone gets into a plank position. The goal is not competition.

Hold until you reach your personal limit.

As people begin to rest, remind the group:

You don't have to be strong enough.

Everyone eventually reaches a physical limit. That isn't failure—it reminds us that we are limited and need God.

Alternative

Use a wall sit or farmer's carry. The same principle applies: recognize your limit and depend on strength beyond yourself.
