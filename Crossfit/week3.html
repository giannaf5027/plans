WEEK 03

Lay Aside Every Weight

What Are You Carrying?

Not everything that weighs us down is necessarily sinful. Some things are simply distractions and hindrances.

Scripture

  • Hebrews 12:1
  • Matthew 11:28–30
  • 1 Peter 5:7
  • Psalm 55:22
  • Philippians 4:6–7
  • Colossians 3:5–10

Possible Weights

Comparison Perfectionism People-Pleasing Fear Academic Pressure Social Media Busyness Shame Control Distraction

Discussion Questions

  1. What does Hebrews mean by “every hindrance” or “weight”?
  2. Why distinguish between weights and sin?
  3. What things aren't necessarily sinful but can distract us from Christ?
  4. What weights are especially common among college students?
  5. How can comparison become a spiritual weight?
  6. How can perfectionism interfere with our relationship with God?
  7. Why can letting go sometimes be harder than holding on?
  8. What are you carrying that God may never have asked you to carry?
  9. What does Matthew 11:28–30 teach about bringing our burdens to Jesus?
  10. If you could physically put down one weight tonight, what would it be?

FIT — OUTDOOR

🎒 The Weight Walk

Give everyone a light backpack, book, water bottle, or other safe object to carry.

  1. Walk while carrying the object.
  2. Stop and silently identify one thing weighing you down.
  3. Physically put the object down.
  4. Continue walking without it.
Sometimes strength means knowing what needs to be laid down.

Alternative

Complete 10 weighted squats followed by 10 bodyweight squats. Discuss the difference.
