WEEK 08

Finish the Race

Faithful to the End

We began with the Cross. We end by asking what it means to remain faithful to Christ for the rest of the race.

Scripture

  • 2 Timothy 4:7
  • Hebrews 12:1–2
  • Philippians 3:13–14
  • 1 Corinthians 15:58
  • Revelation 2:10
  • Matthew 25:21

Discussion Questions

  1. What does Paul mean when he says, “I have finished the race”?
  2. What's the difference between finishing successfully and finishing faithfully?
  3. Why is starting something often easier than finishing it?
  4. What can cause Christians to lose sight of Jesus over time?
  5. What does faithfulness look like when you aren't seeing results?
  6. What does Hebrews 12 tell us to do when we're tired?
  7. What does it mean to keep your eyes on Jesus all the way to the finish?
  8. What kind of Christian do you want to become over the next 5, 10, or 20 years?
  9. If someone described your faith at the end of your life, what would you hope they said?
  10. What is one commitment you want to make to Christ as you continue your race?

FINAL FIT

🏁 The Cross Finish Line

Set up a finish line with a cross at the end. Complete four symbolic stations.

✝️ 1 — Surrender

10 squats

What do I need to surrender?

🎒 2 — Endurance

30-second wall sit

Where do I need to keep going?

👀 3 — Focus

30-second balance

Am I looking at Jesus?

🤝 4 — Community

Partner exercise

Who am I running with?

The Finish

Everyone crosses the finish line together.

“I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.” — 2 Timothy 4:7

Close by returning to the original theme:

Lay aside every weight. Run with endurance. Keep your eyes on Jesus.
