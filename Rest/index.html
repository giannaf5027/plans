TRUE REST

Resting
in God

Learning to slow down, be still, and abide in Christ.

A Bible study for Christian college women exploring what it means to find true rest—not simply by stopping our activity, but by finding our peace, identity, and security in God.

THE IDEA

What Is True Rest?

Our culture often treats rest as something we earn after accomplishing enough. We work, study, serve, socialize, and accomplish—and only then do we feel permitted to slow down.

Scripture presents a different picture.

True rest is not simply the absence of activity. It is learning to trust God enough to stop striving for our worth, our security, and our control.

"My soul finds rest in God alone."

Psalm 62:1
