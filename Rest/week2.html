WEEK 02

Sabbath Rest

Finding Peace in God's Rhythm

Learning to trust God enough to step away from productivity and receive the gift of rest.

KEY SCRIPTURES

Genesis 2:2–3 & Hebrews 4:9–10

DISCUSSION

Rest & Productivity

  1. What messages has culture taught you about rest and productivity?
  2. What fears come up when you think about slowing down or unplugging?
  3. How does constant productivity affect your soul, relationships, and faith?
  4. Why do you think God intentionally designed rest into creation?
  5. What do you think it means to trust God enough to rest?
  6. How can rest become a spiritual practice rather than just a break?
  7. How could Sabbath reshape the way you view your worth and identity?
  8. What boundaries might be necessary to protect rhythms of rest?

ACTIVITY

Create a "Rest" Plan

Brainstorm ways to incorporate Sabbath-like rhythms into your busy life—even if it begins with an afternoon or a few intentional hours each week.

Consider:

  • When can I unplug?
  • What activities actually restore me?
  • What activities make me feel more exhausted?
  • How can I make space for prayer?
  • What boundaries would protect this time?
  • What am I afraid will happen if I stop?

Final Reflection

What is one small, concrete way you could practice rest in God this week?
