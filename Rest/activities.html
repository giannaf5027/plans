ACTIVITY LIBRARY

Practice
Rest

Activities that help turn the ideas of True Rest into practices we can actually experience.

01 — SILENCE

Silent Prayer

Spend 5–15 minutes sitting quietly before God. No phones. No conversation. No music.

Begin with:

"Lord, I am here. Help me to be present with You."

If distractions arise, acknowledge them and gently return your attention to God.

02 — SCRIPTURE

Scripture Meditation

  1. Choose a short passage.
  2. Read it slowly.
  3. Read it again.
  4. Identify one word or phrase that stands out.
  5. Sit with that phrase.
  6. Journal what God may be teaching you.

03 — OUTDOOR

Prayer Walk

Take a slow walk together without rushing.

Assign different sections of the walk to different prayer prompts:

  • Thank God for something.
  • Confess something.
  • Pray for someone else.
  • Ask God for wisdom.
  • Spend a few minutes simply noticing His creation.

04 — OUTDOOR

Creation Reflection

Go somewhere with trees, water, gardens, or another peaceful natural environment.

Spend several minutes observing without trying to accomplish anything.

Read Psalm 23 or Psalm 46 afterward.

05 — REFLECTION

Pour Out Your Heart

Give each person 10 minutes to write honestly to God.

Consider these prompts:

  • What am I carrying?
  • What am I trying to control?
  • What am I afraid to surrender?
  • Where am I exhausted?
  • What do I need from God?

06 — PRACTICAL

Build a Rest Plan

Daily

Choose one small practice of intentional time with God.

Weekly

Choose a block of time to intentionally unplug and rest.

Monthly

Schedule a longer period of reflection, solitude, or time in creation.

07 — COMMUNITY

Rest Together

Rest does not always mean being alone. Plan a low-pressure gathering centered around simply being together.

  • Picnic
  • Walk
  • Coffee and conversation
  • Worship night
  • Scripture reading
  • Sunset or sunrise reflection

08 — BAD WEATHER

Indoor Alternatives

  • Guided prayer
  • Scripture meditation
  • Journaling
  • Worship and reflection
  • Quiet coloring / drawing
  • Phone-free tea or coffee conversation
  • Lectio Divina
  • Personal prayer stations

Rest Is a Practice.

The goal is not simply to know that God offers rest.

The goal is to learn how to receive it.
