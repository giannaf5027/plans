WEEK 07

Restoring
Our Souls

Reflecting on the Journey

Looking back at what God has taught us about stillness, intimacy, Scripture, Sabbath, and finding rest in Him.

KEY SCRIPTURES

Psalm 23 & Matthew 11:28–30

"He restores my soul."

Psalm 23:3

GROUP REFLECTION

Looking Back

  1. Looking back, what has changed in the way you view stillness or time with God?
  2. What has God revealed to you about yourself during this season?
  3. Where have you noticed growth, even if it feels small?
  4. What practices or perspectives do you want to carry forward?
  5. What obstacles might challenge you after this group ends?
  6. How can community help you remain intentional about slowing down?
  7. If you could describe this journey in one word or phrase, what would it be and why?

REFLECTION ACTIVITY

Remember & Respond

Part One — Remember

Write down three things God has taught you during this study.

Part Two — Recognize

Identify one area where you have seen God working in your life.

Part Three — Respond

Choose one practice you want to continue after the study ends.

What Will You Carry Forward?

True rest is not simply something we experienced during these seven weeks.

It is a way of returning to God again and again.
