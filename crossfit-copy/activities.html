✝️ Keep the FIT Meaningful

The physical activity should never become exercise for exercise's sake. The goal is to make the physical experience reinforce the biblical message.

When choosing a replacement activity, ask:

  1. What is the biblical theme?
  2. What physical action represents that theme?
  3. What can the group physically experience?
  4. What question can we ask immediately afterward?
Don't just exercise while thinking about the Bible study. Make the exercise itself teach the Bible study.
