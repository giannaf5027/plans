WEEK 02

The Marathon

It's Not a Sprint

Following Jesus is a lifelong race of endurance, not a sprint toward a quick finish.

Big Idea

The Christian life is a marathon. We aren't called to move as quickly as possible, but to remain faithful to Jesus over the long haul.

Scripture

  • Hebrews 12:1–2 — Run with endurance.
  • 1 Corinthians 9:24–27
  • Philippians 3:12–14
  • Galatians 6:9
  • 2 Timothy 4:7
  • James 1:12

Discussion Questions

  1. Why does Scripture compare the Christian life to a race?
  2. What is the difference between a sprint and a marathon in regards to a Christian life?
  3. Why might we sometimes treat Christianity like a sprint?
  4. What does it mean to “run with endurance”?
  5. Why is endurance necessary if we know where the race ends?
  6. What causes people to lose endurance in their faith?
  7. What does endurance look like when you don't feel close to God?
  8. Where are you tempted to rush God's timing?
  9. What area of your life currently requires patience and perseverance?
  10. If your relationship with Jesus is a marathon, what is one thing you need to stop rushing?

FIT — OUTDOOR

🌸 The Hot Girl Walk

Take a 5–10 minute walk outside. The goal isn't speed. The goal is endurance.

First 2 Minutes

Walk silently and consider:

What race has God placed before me?

Middle

Walk with a partner and discuss:

Where do you need endurance right now?

Final Minutes

Walk silently while repeating: “Keep your eyes on Jesus.”

Connection

We could try to rush to the finish line, but the Christian life isn't a sprint. We're learning to faithfully keep walking toward Christ.

🌧️ Bad Weather Alternative

Walk laps around a church, gym, student center, or other indoor space.
