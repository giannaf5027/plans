WEEK 07

Run Together

Christianity Was Never Meant to Be Solo

Scripture

  • Galatians 6:2
  • Ecclesiastes 4:9–12
  • Hebrews 10:24–25
  • Romans 12:4–5
  • 1 Thessalonians 5:11
  • Proverbs 27:17

Discussion Questions

  1. Why did God design Christianity around community?
  2. What does “bear one another's burdens” actually look like?
  3. Why can vulnerability be difficult even with Christian friends?
  4. Are you more comfortable helping people or being helped?
  5. What makes a friendship spiritually healthy?
  6. What does it mean to “spur one another on”?
  7. Who helps you keep your eyes on Jesus?
  8. Are you helping anyone else keep their eyes on Jesus?
  9. What could our group do to become genuine spiritual community?
  10. What is one practical way you can help someone else run their race this week?

FIT — PARTNER WORKOUT

🤝 Nobody Finishes Alone

Partner Squats

Face each other and hold hands while squatting together.

Plank High-Fives

Both partners plank and alternate high-fives.

Partner Sit-Ups

Face each other and high-five at the top of each sit-up.

Partner Resistance

One partner provides gentle resistance while the other walks forward.

If one person needs to slow down, both slow down.

The goal isn't to reach heaven before everyone else. The goal is to help one another remain faithful to Christ.
