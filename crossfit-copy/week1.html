WEEK 01

The Cross

Where Our Race Begins

Before we talk about our race, we have to remember what Christ has already done for us.

Big Idea

Christianity doesn't begin with what we can do for God. It begins with what God has already done for us through Jesus.

We don't train to earn God's love. We train because Christ has already loved us and given Himself for us.

Scripture

Primary

  • Galatians 2:20
  • Luke 9:23
  • 1 Corinthians 1:18

Additional

  • Romans 5:6–8
  • Isaiah 53:4–6
  • John 3:16
  • 1 Peter 2:24
  • Philippians 2:5–8
  • Ephesians 2:8–9

Discussion Questions

  1. When you hear the word “Cross,” what is the first thing that comes to mind?
  2. Why do you think the Cross is the central symbol of Christianity?
  3. What specifically did Jesus accomplish through His death on the Cross?
  4. Why does Paul say the message of the Cross can seem like “foolishness” to the world?
  5. What is the difference between knowing about the Cross and actually living in light of the Cross?
  6. What does Galatians 2:20 mean when Paul says, “I have been crucified with Christ”?
  7. What does Jesus mean by “take up your cross daily”?
  8. Are there areas where you are trying to earn God's approval rather than trusting His grace?
  9. What competes with Jesus for first place in your life?
  10. If the Cross became the true center of your life, what might change about the way you approach this semester?

FIT — 5–10 MINUTES

✝️ The Cross Hold

Stand with your arms extended horizontally, forming the shape of a cross.

Activity

  1. Hold the Cross position for 30–60 seconds.
  2. Complete 10 squats.
  3. Complete 10 alternating lunges.
  4. Complete 5–10 push-ups.
  5. Return to the Cross position.

Reflection

This physical discomfort is obviously nothing compared with what Christ endured. But let it remind us that Jesus willingly endured suffering on our behalf.

Question: Because Christ gave Himself for us, what are we willing to surrender to Him?

Outdoor Option

Finish with a five-minute silent walk while reflecting: “What has Christ done for me?”

🌧️ Bad Weather Alternative

Complete the Cross Hold and exercises indoors. The physical symbolism remains the same.
